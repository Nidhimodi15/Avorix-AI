Years ago, a simple "Contact Us" form on your website was enough. In 2026, consumer patience is at an all-time low. If a prospect can't reach you on their preferred platform—instantly—they are out. 

To thrive today, local businesses need an **Omnichannel Lead Capture Strategy** powered by automation.

### What is Omnichannel Lead Capture?
Omnichannel means being everywhere your customer is, but managing everything from one place. Whether someone DMs your Instagram, sends a WhatsApp message, or drops their email on your custom-built site, the experience must be unified.

### The Problem with Scattered Channels
If John messages you on Facebook, Sarah emails you, and Mike texts you on WhatsApp, your sales team is jumping between three different apps. Leads slip through the cracks. Responses are delayed. Money is left on the table.

### How Automation Solves the Puzzle
By implementing intelligent integration platforms (like those built by Aeviq AI), we pipe every single inquiry into one centralized dashboard. 

Here is how it works:
1. **Instant Response:** An AI chatbot immediately replies to the prospect on the platform they used, answering their basic questions.
2. **Qualification:** The bot asks a few quick questions to determine if they are a hot lead.
3. **CRM Injection:** The lead's details are instantly saved into your CRM.
4. **Sales Alert:** Your sales team gets a single ping on their dashboard saying: *"Hot lead from WhatsApp ready to close."*
