If you run a restaurant, cloud kitchen, or food delivery business in India, here is a number that should change how you think about marketing: **WhatsApp messages in India have a 98% open rate.**

Email? 18–22%. SMS? 15–25%. Instagram posts? 2–5% of followers see them.

WhatsApp is the only channel where you can reach virtually every customer who has opted in — instantly, personally, and for a fraction of a rupee per message.

## Why WhatsApp Works So Well for Restaurants

Restaurants are uniquely suited to WhatsApp marketing because:

1. **Food is visual and emotional** — a photo of your special thali or new dessert triggers instant craving
2. **Decisions are fast** — people decide where to eat within minutes
3. **Repeat customers are your biggest revenue** — WhatsApp keeps you top-of-mind between visits
4. **Local reach is everything** — WhatsApp groups and broadcasts naturally spread within neighbourhoods

## Setting Up WhatsApp Automation for Your Restaurant

Here is how to automate your restaurant's WhatsApp marketing properly:

### Step 1: Build Your Customer List (Legally)
Collect opt-ins through:
- A QR code on every table linking to a WhatsApp opt-in page
- A sign at the counter: "Get exclusive deals on WhatsApp — scan here"
- Your Zomato/Swiggy delivery packaging with a sticker QR code

**Important:** Only message people who have explicitly opted in. Sending unsolicited messages violates WhatsApp policy and your messages will be reported.

### Step 2: Segment Your List
Not all customers are the same. Create groups:
- **Regular dine-in customers** — promotions and table booking nudges
- **Delivery customers** — combo deals and new menu items
- **Birthday list** — automated birthday greetings with offers
- **Lapsed customers (30+ days)** — re-engagement campaigns

### Step 3: Set Up Automation Triggers
With a WhatsApp Business API setup:
- New customer order → automatic welcome message + opt-in confirmation
- 7 days after last order → "We miss you" message
- Birthday (from your database) → automatic birthday greeting
- 10 AM every day → daily special broadcast to opted-in list

### Step 4: Track What Works
Monitor:
- **Message open rate** (target: 90%+)
- **Response rate** (how many reply)
- **Conversion rate** (how many visit or order after a message)
- **Opt-out rate** (if this rises, your content is too frequent or irrelevant)
