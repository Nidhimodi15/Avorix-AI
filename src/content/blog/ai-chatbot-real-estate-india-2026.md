If you are a real estate agent or developer in India, you already know the pain: a prospect messages you at 10 PM asking about a 2BHK in Bopal, Ahmedabad — and by the time you reply the next morning, they have already booked a site visit with someone else.

## The Indian Real Estate Lead Problem

Property enquiries in India do not follow office hours. MagicBricks, 99acres, and Housing.com ads run 24/7, and leads expect instant responses. Research shows:

- **78% of buyers** choose the first agent who responds
- Average response time for Indian real estate agents: **4–6 hours**
- Leads contacted within **5 minutes** are 100x more likely to convert than those contacted after 30 minutes

The gap between "enquiry received" and "first response" is where most deals die.

## What a Real Estate AI Chatbot Does

A well-built WhatsApp AI chatbot for a real estate business handles the entire first stage of your sales funnel automatically:

### 1. Instant Welcome & Qualification
The moment a lead fills your form on 99acres or clicks your WhatsApp link, the bot immediately:
- Greets them by name
- Asks: budget range, location preference, BHK requirement, timeline to buy
- Tags them as Hot / Warm / Cold based on answers

### 2. Sends Brochures & Floor Plans
Based on their preferences, the bot instantly sends the right project brochure, floor plan PDF, and photo gallery — without any manual work from your team.

### 3. Books Site Visits
The bot checks your Google Calendar and offers 3 available time slots. The prospect picks one. Done. The appointment appears in your calendar automatically.

### 4. Follow-Up Sequences
If a lead goes cold, the bot sends a follow-up after 2 days, then again after 5 days with a different angle (new project launch, limited inventory, price revision). **No lead falls through the cracks.**
