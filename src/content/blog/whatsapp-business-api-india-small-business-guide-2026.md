## What is the Difference Between WhatsApp Business App and WhatsApp Business API?

**WhatsApp Business App** (the free one you download):
- Works on one device only
- No automation possible
- Maximum of 256 people in a broadcast list
- Cannot integrate with your CRM or website

**WhatsApp Business API** (what we set up for clients):
- Works with multiple agents simultaneously
- Full automation — bots reply 24/7 without you being online
- Unlimited broadcast contacts (with opt-in)
- Integrates directly with your CRM, booking system, or Shopify store
- Required for running proper WhatsApp marketing campaigns

## How Much Does WhatsApp Business API Cost in India?

WhatsApp charges per conversation, not per message. Here is how the pricing works in 2026:

| Conversation Type | Cost per Conversation (INR) |
|---|---|
| User-initiated (customer messages you first) | ₹0.00 (Free) |
| Business-initiated marketing message | ₹0.78 — ₹1.10 |
| Business-initiated utility message | ₹0.14 — ₹0.20 |

**What does this mean practically?**

If you send a promotional WhatsApp broadcast to 1,000 customers, it costs approximately **₹780 — ₹1,100 total**. Compare this to SMS (₹0.10–₹0.25 per SMS with a 20% open rate) versus WhatsApp (₹0.78–₹1.10 per conversation with a **98% open rate**). WhatsApp wins by a massive margin on ROI.

## How to Get WhatsApp Business API Approved in India

Getting API access yourself is a 3–4 week process involving Meta's Business Verification. Here are the steps:

1. **Register on Meta Business Suite** — Verify your business with GST number or incorporation documents
2. **Apply for the WhatsApp Business Platform** — Submit your business phone number (must be a fresh number not used on any WhatsApp account before)
3. **Wait for Meta Approval** — Takes 1–3 weeks
4. **Connect a BSP (Business Solution Provider)** — Companies like Interakt, Wati, or our custom solution at Aeviq AI
