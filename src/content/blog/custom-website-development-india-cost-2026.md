## Template vs. Custom Website: What's the Real Difference?

This is the first question most business owners get wrong.

**Template websites (Wix, Squarespace, WordPress themes):**
- Built on a pre-designed framework
- Looks like thousands of other websites
- Limited customisation beyond colours and text
- Difficult to integrate with Indian payment gateways, CRMs, or WhatsApp
- Average cost: ₹5,000–₹30,000

**Custom websites:**
- Designed and built specifically for your brand and business goals
- Unique look that competitors cannot replicate
- Integrates with any tool — Razorpay, WhatsApp API, Zoho CRM, booking systems
- Fast loading, fully SEO-optimised from day one
- Average cost: ₹40,000–₹3,00,000+

The difference is not just aesthetics. A custom website built for conversion can generate 2–5x more leads from the same traffic.

## What You Should Get in a Custom Website Package

A proper custom website build should always include:

✅ Custom UI/UX design (not a template)  
✅ Mobile-first responsive design  
✅ Google PageSpeed score of 90+ (critical for SEO)  
✅ Basic on-page SEO (meta tags, schema markup, sitemap)  
✅ SSL certificate (HTTPS)  
✅ Contact forms connected to your email or WhatsApp  
✅ Google Analytics setup  
✅ 1–3 months of post-launch support  

If an agency is not offering all of these, you are not getting a complete product.

## Red Flags When Hiring a Website Developer in India

Watch out for these:

🚩 **"We will deliver in 3 days"** — Custom websites cannot be built properly in 3 days. This means templates.

🚩 **No contract or scope document** — Always get a written breakdown of what is included before paying.

🚩 **They own your domain and hosting** — Your domain and hosting should always be in your name, not the agency's.

🚩 **No post-launch support mentioned** — Bugs happen. Make sure support is included.

🚩 **No portfolio of similar work** — Ask to see websites they have built for businesses like yours.

## SEO and Websites: Why This Combination Matters

A beautiful website that no one finds is worthless. In 2026, Google ranks websites based on:

1. **Page speed** — especially on mobile (3G speeds are still common in Tier 2/3 India)
2. **Core Web Vitals** — Google's measure of user experience
3. **Content quality** — not keyword stuffing, but genuinely helpful content
4. **Local relevance** — mentioning your city, services, and India-specific context
5. **Schema markup** — structured data that helps Google understand your business
